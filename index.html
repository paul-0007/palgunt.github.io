About Me

I am a Project Associate at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, currently working on molecular communication, Internet of NanoBio Things, and targeted drug delivery.

My current work involves exploring computational models of molecular transport and developing initial simulation frameworks based on stochastic particle dynamics, including three-dimensional Brownian motion and Monte Carlo methods.

I hold an M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence from the Defence Institute of Advanced Technology (DIAT-DRDO), where my research focused on computer vision, spatiotemporal learning, explainable AI, anomaly detection, and real-time AI systems.

I am interested in applying computational intelligence, machine learning, and simulation methods to complex physical and biomedical systems.
